Ask a question
Is there really no staff in the store?

Sales and payment are fully automatic; nobody stands at a till. Staff only visit at set intervals for restocking and cleaning. In the Full Operation model we plan and carry out those visits too.

How much space is needed?

Lobby Store is modular; it scales from a small lobby corner to a large store area. In the discovery meeting we measure your space and prepare a layout plan specific to it.

Can I buy the software on its own?

Lobby Store's strength comes from design, hardware and software working together, so we install the system as a whole. If you already have a store, we can look at conversion options together.

Who decides the product range?

In the Turnkey model, entirely you. In the Full Operation model we handle category management; we keep improving the shelf based on sales data and build a range that suits your audience.

Can I open in more than one location?

Yes. All your stores are managed from the same panel; prices, promotions and reports are controlled from a single centre.

How long does installation take, and what does it cost?

Both depend on the size of the area and the model you choose. After the discovery meeting we give you a clear timeline and price quote, with no surprise items.
