Frequently asked

Frequently asked
questions.

For matters not covered here, please contact us.

Contact us
Are there staff in the store?

Sales and payment are fully automated; there are no checkout staff. Visits are made at set intervals for restocking and cleaning only. In the Full Operation model, these visits are planned and carried out by Lobby Store.

How much space is required?

Lobby Store has a modular structure; it scales from a small lobby area to a large store area. During the discovery meeting the space is measured and a layout plan specific to the premises is prepared.

Is the software available separately?

Lobby Store is an integrated system in which design, hardware and software work together, and it is therefore installed as a whole. Conversion options for existing stores are assessed separately.

How is the product range determined?

In the Turnkey model, the product range is determined entirely by the operator. In the Full Operation model, category management is carried out by Lobby Store; the range is updated regularly in line with sales data and the user profile of the premises.

Can stores be installed in more than one location?

Yes. All stores are managed through the same panel; pricing, promotions and reporting are controlled centrally.

What are the installation time and cost?

Time and cost vary according to the size of the area and the model selected. A detailed project timeline and price quote are provided following the discovery meeting.
